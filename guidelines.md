# Course
Deep Learning — Master's (6 credits)

## Goal
Solve a practical problem using deep learning, end-to-end. Go deeper than the labs. Use PyTorch or TensorFlow, and the VSC supercomputer for training.

## Group
2 students.

## Deadlines & Deliverables
|Date	|Deliverable|
|-------|----------------|
|16 Oct (midnight)	|Proposal — max 1 page: what problem, how you'd solve it|
|21 Oct	|Coach feedback session (15 min discussion)|
|13 Nov	|Intermediate update submission|
|18 Nov	|Intermediate discussion with coach|
|15 Dec	|Poster submission (A1 format, printed)|
|16 Dec	|Poster presentation + feedback|
|4 Jan	|Rebuttal + final report (max 5 pages, template provided)|


## Intermediate Milestone
- Show you're on the right track.
- Have read up on the problem and already tried some methods.
- Be ready to discuss problems you're hitting.

## Poster Session
- Present problem statement, what you tried, results, conclusions.
- Printed in A1.
- Keep track of all feedback — you must address it in the final report.


## Final Report
- Max 5 pages (template provided).
- Explicitly state what you changed following poster feedback.


## Evaluation Criteria
1. Correctness — sound implementation and design choices.
2. Mastery — can discuss the topic well and in detail.
3. Ambition — how challenging the problem is.
4. Novelty — try new things, don't just copy existing methods.


## Rules
- No reusing results from other deep learning course projects.
- Pick a topic you want to master — something you can boast about on your CV.
